Reel Poster - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Reel Poster is a personal tool used only by its owner, Ryan D (Digital Garage). It does not collect data from any other person.

What is stored: the owner's own login tokens for the owner's own accounts (such as TikTok), kept only on the owner's own computer.

What is shared: nothing. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere except to the platform the owner is posting to.

Deleting data: the owner can disconnect an account at any time, which removes its stored token.

Questions? Contact the owner through the Digital Garage accounts on TikTok, Instagram or YouTube.
